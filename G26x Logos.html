G26x · Logo package

Three marks, four variants each

Monogram, Editorial Wordmark and Tech Modern. Each has Dark and Light variants for brand-colour reproduction, plus Black and White single-ink variants for print, embroidery, favicons and subdued applications.

Logos are exempt

The marks and the taglines keep their unique treatment — including the serif display face in the Editorial Wordmark. That carve-out is explicit and it stops at the artwork: this page, and every surface that surrounds a logo, follows the Apple HIG standard like everything else.

The proof grounds below are fixed black and white on purpose. A proof that changed with the page theme would not be a proof.
