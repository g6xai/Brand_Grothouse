04Usage Rules

Five rules never broken.

  1. Italic serif is the accent. Use italic Source Serif 4 for exactly one weight-bearing phrase per composition. Never italicize body copy for emphasis — use weight instead.
  2. Uppercase lives in mono. If text is ALL CAPS, it is set in JetBrains Mono, 10–11px, letter-spacing: 0.28em. Never set uppercase in the serif or sans.
  3. Display wraps short. Hero and H1 copy maxes out at ≈20 characters per line. Use max-width: 20ch and text-wrap: balance.
  4. Numbers are serifs too. Large numeric displays (AUM, prices, stats) use Source Serif 4, not the sans — italicize the modifier (e.g. .82) for emphasis.
  5. Measure is 56–62ch. Body paragraphs cap at max-width: 62ch. Leads sit at 56ch. Nothing wider, no exceptions.
✓ Do
  • Use italic serif for accent phrases: "built on faith"
  • Mix weights (400 body, 700 display) to build hierarchy.
  • Track mono eyebrows to 0.28em, uppercase, 11px max.
  • Use Source Serif 4 for large stat displays.
  • Cap line length at 62ch with text-wrap: pretty.
✕ Don't
  • Substitute Playfair, Lora, or any other serif.
  • Use italic in body sans for emphasis — use weight.
  • Track the serif headline. It is drawn, not tracked.
  • Set buttons or form labels in the serif.
  • Use more than three font families on a single page.
05Preset Variations

Same families, five voices.

Each entity can render in one of five type presets without introducing new families. The preset decides which of the three families leads the composition.

PresetDisplayBodyAccent behavior
editorialSource Serif 4 · 700Outfit · 400Italic serif on one phrase. The canonical default.
brutalistJetBrains Mono · 700 UPPERJetBrains Mono · 400No italic. Tracking alone creates rhythm. 0-radius surfaces.
glassSource Serif 4 · 700Outfit · 300Lighter weight body over translucent cards. Italic accent remains.
minimalOutfit · 600Outfit · 400Sans-only. Hierarchy from weight + whitespace, no serif.
maximalistSource Serif 4 · 700 @ 1.25× scaleOutfit · 400Oversized serif display. Italic at display size. Gradient accent.

Most marketing surfaces should stay on editorial. Reach for brutalist for developer-tech surfaces, minimal for high-density data apps, maximalist for major announcements.

06Print & Collateral

Ink on paper.

The typographic system was designed print-first. All three families render cleanly in offset and digital print at any reasonable size.

  • Body — 10–11pt Outfit Regular, leading 14–15pt, measure 60–68 characters.
  • Display — Source Serif 4 Bold, 36–72pt depending on format. Italic cuts for accent phrases.
  • Caption / metadata — 8pt JetBrains Mono Medium, tracked 0.22em if uppercase.
  • Minimum print body — 9pt. Never smaller. Footnotes may drop to 8pt in annual reports only.
  • Letterhead — Wordmark 24mm, 50mm top margin, 20mm side margins. Body starts at the golden-section line.
  • Business card — 85×55mm. Monogram top-left (8mm). Name in Outfit Medium 9pt. Title in JetBrains Mono 7pt, tracked 0.18em.
